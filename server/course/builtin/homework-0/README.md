# Homework 0 — Using your tutor

*Start here, before homework 1.*

Before you build anything, get to know the tutor you will build it with:
the course rail, the lesson page and your coach. You pass this homework the
way you will pass every homework after it — one Rule at a time, with your
coach checking off each Example once you have seen it work.

## How a homework works

Each homework is a set of feature files in Gherkin. A **Rule** says
something that must be true; each **Example** under it is one concrete case
of it. Your coach puts one Rule in focus at a time and works through its
Examples with you. When an Example works, the coach checks it off, and the
course rail keeps count.

In this homework the Examples are about the tutor itself, so there is
nothing to build. Do what each Example says, then tell your coach what you
saw; that is the evidence. If something does not happen the way the Example
says, tell the coach that too. It will mark the Example *not yet* and help
you work out why.

## What to look for

- The **course rail** on the left: your homework, the Rules in it, and your
  conversations with the coach.
- The **lesson page**: the homework on paper, with your conversation with
  the coach beneath it.
- **Progress cards** and **term chips** in the coach's replies.

When every Example has passed, the coach closes the homework and homework 1
is ready to start.
