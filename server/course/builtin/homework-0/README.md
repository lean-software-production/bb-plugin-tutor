# Lesson 0 — Using your tutor

*Start here, before lesson 1.*

Before you build anything, get to know the tutor you will build it with:
the course outline, your coach thread and its side chats. You pass this
lesson the way you will pass every lesson after it — one Rule at a time,
with your coach checking off each Example once you have seen it work.

## How a lesson works

Each lesson is a set of feature files in Gherkin. A **Rule** says
something that must be true; each **Example** under it is one concrete case
of it. Your coach puts one Rule in focus at a time and works through its
Examples with you. When an Example works, the coach checks it off, and the
course outline keeps count.

In this lesson the Examples are about the tutor itself, so there is nothing
to build. Do what each Example says, then tell your coach what you saw;
that is the evidence. If something does not happen the way the Example
says, tell the coach that too. It will mark the Example *not yet* and help
you work out why.

## What to look for

- The **course outline** in the sidebar: every lesson, your coach thread
  and side chats under the lesson you are on, and the Rules your coach
  works through.
- Your **coach thread**: it opens with the lesson card, and each Rule
  starts at its Rule card.
- **Side chats** for side questions, in the thread's right panel.
- **Progress cards** and **term chips** in the coach's replies.

When every Example has passed, the coach closes the lesson and lesson 1 is
ready to start.
